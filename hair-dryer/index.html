This policy explains what data the Android app Hair Dryer Sounds: White Noise (com.iYeahEntertainment.HairDryer), published on Google Play by Warriani Entertainment, collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The app has no sign-in and no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your settings (for example the chosen sound, timer or animal) are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described below.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Data collected through advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your consent choices and region, the SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance.

This data is used for advertising (including ad personalization where you allow it), analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice later from the app's menu (Privacy settings). You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · AdMob and user data.

Permissions

Internet and network state are used to load ads. Any other permission is described above and is used only for the feature it belongs to.

Data retention and deletion

We do not store your data on our servers. Settings and any files the app keeps stay on your device and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google through ads is handled under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and Android ad settings. You may also email us with any request about your data and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS). The app does not transmit other data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
